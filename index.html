Services

From survey to certified line marking

Kanstet designs and builds indoor and outdoor athletic facilities from A to Z, and installs any brand of surface. We aren't tied to a single manufacturer.

  1. Track layout & drawings

    AutoCAD construction drawings for every IAAF construction category. Tell us the level of competition you'll host; standard 400 m outdoor and 200 m indoor layouts are available on request.

  2. Topographic survey & stake-out

    The IAAF allows almost no tolerance on running distance, so we set reference points with robotic total stations from day one.

  3. Civil works supervision

    Technical supervision of earthworks, stone base and asphalt or concrete paving, because the surface is only as good as what's under it.

  4. Surface installation

    Full PU, sandwich and spray-coat systems, semi-prefabricated, in-situ or roll-out, installed by crews who know how each manufacturer's material behaves.

  5. Line marking & IAAF survey

    Precision line marking with Trimble instruments and our own lightweight lining machines, plus survey reports for IAAF track certification. Kanstet has surveyed certified tracks in Bulgaria, Brazil, India, Poland, Latvia and Nigeria.

About

Built by athletes, for athletes

Kanstet was founded in 1992 by members of Estonia's national Olympic team, with a single focus: track & field. Since its first track in 1999, Kanstet has built outdoor and indoor tracks, turf fields and courts across Europe, Asia and Africa. That includes the new surface at Al-Shaab Stadium in Baghdad and the arena for the 2013 World Youth Championships in Donetsk.

Kanstet USA brings that experience to American schools, universities, clubs and meet organizers, with local sales and support based in Texas and Idaho.
